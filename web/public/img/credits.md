## Notes for the designer

- **hero.jpg** already has a dark left side, so white Roboto text will read well on it even under a light veil. Its sand and brown tones sit close to #baa474 and #483725. Use `background-position: left center` so the dark water stays behind the headline when the screen is narrow.
- **field.jpg** is portrait. For a wide band, crop it with `object-fit: cover; object-position: center 60%` so the workers and the water stay in frame. Neither face is clearly identifiable: one is hidden by a hood and a hard hat, and the other is a small, partly turned profile.
- **lab.jpg** shows handwritten German sample labels and a tiny printed product code on the cartridges. There is no brand logo big enough to read once the veil is on.
- **aerial.jpg** is the only photo that shows recognisable Perth, because the CBD skyline is visible on the horizon.
- The images contain no firefighting foam, no Western Environmental imagery and no AI-generated images. Each one is a normal camera or drone photo from the photographer's own page.

## Candidates I rejected, and why

- **A gloved hand taking a water sample** (first choice for the hero): every close match on Unsplash is an Unsplash+ (Getty Images) premium photo. Those need a paid licence, so I did not use them. I found no free, wide, gloved-hand water-sampling photo on Unsplash or Pexels.
- **Tampa Bay Estuary Program, "a woman crouches down to collect water from a pond"** (https://unsplash.com/photos/dHXw8yEEpew): the caption says water collection, but at full size the photo shows a man planting a rush at a Florida wetland, and his face is clearly identifiable. I dropped it and used the WA aerial instead, which was the third choice for the hero.
- **A slug test at a monitoring bore** (Unsplash photo-1720026837942-243519f0d410): very on topic, but it is posed at a mine site with a readable site signboard and an identifiable face.
